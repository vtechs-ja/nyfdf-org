Who We Are

Rooted in purpose.
Committed to people.

The Northgate Youth and Family Development Foundation (NYFDF) is an expression of a vision to participate in the process of nation building in a constructive and meaningful way. We believe that successful nation building requires a deliberate focus on the strategic development of young people — and the family structures to which they belong.

Growing out of more than ten years of youth and family development initiatives through the Northgate Centre for Global Impact, NYFDF was incorporated as a charitable organisation under the Companies Act of Jamaica on July 2, 2008. Our work spans education, health outreach, counselling, technology access, parenting empowerment, and sustained community development across Jamaica.

We also partner with corporate entities on organisational policy, team culture, and strategic development — bringing the same values-based framework to institutional work that guides our community programmes.

Character & Leadership Development

School-based seminars and community programmes equipping young people with leadership skills, moral values, and a sense of civic responsibility.

02

Parenting Empowerment

Workshops and mentoring programmes — including the Northgate Parent Empowerment Place (N-PEP) — equipping parents and caregivers with tools to raise purpose-driven children.

Health & Medical Outreach

Community medical clinics and health fairs delivering free screening, dental services, nutritional counselling, and health education — including the annual Northgate Clinic and Operation Grace.

04

Project Esther

A ten-week character development programme providing mentoring, counselling, and activation for young women — including those transitioning out of state residential facilities at age eighteen.

05

Technology Access — C-TEC

An NCTVET-accredited Community Technological Empowerment Centre offering certified Data Operations training, digital literacy, and technology access for individuals, youth, and organisations.

06

Financial Literacy — Youth $$ Programme

Delivered in collaboration with The Consulting Interface (Trinidad & Tobago), this programme exposes young people to money management, enterprise, and economic citizenship.

Our Leadership

Wesley Curwin Boynes, JP
Founder & Chairman  ·  Justice of the Peace, St. Mary

Born in San Fernando, Trinidad and Tobago, Wesley Boynes has spent more than two decades in strategic leadership, community development, and human capacity building across Jamaica. He serves as Senior Elder at Philadelphia Life Centre, has been a Justice of the Peace for St. Mary since 2018, and is a current board leader in Jamaican independent education. Current Flagship Initiative

Operation
Grace

Launched in response to Hurricane Melissa, Operation Grace is NYFDF's 2026 community recovery programme — a six-month series of monthly outreach events serving communities in Cambridge St. James.

Each edition is themed around a specific need — from general health screening to male empowerment, women's wellness, and youth development. Every event integrates clinical care, counselling, recreation, digital access, and care packages.

Operation Grace reflects what NYFDF has always done: show up consistently, serve holistically, and treat every person with dignity.
