Who We Are

Rooted in community.
Driven by purpose.

The Northgate Youth and Family Development Foundation (NYFDF) was established to address the systemic gaps in health, social support, and opportunity that affect youth and families across Jamaica. We believe that lasting change begins at the community level — and that every person deserves access to care, dignity, and the tools to thrive.

Our work spans health outreach, youth development, family counselling, disaster response, and community mobilisation. We partner with government agencies, health professionals, civil society, and corporate sponsors to deliver programmes that are not only responsive — but sustained.

What We Do

Programmes &
Initiatives

Our programming is designed to meet communities at their point of need. From clinical health outreach to youth engagement and digital access — every initiative is shaped by the people it serves.

01

Community Health Outreach

Free clinical screenings, health education, vaccinations, and linkage to care delivered directly into underserved communities.

02

Operation Grace

A 6-month monthly outreach programme serving communities affected by Hurricane Melissa, with targeted editions for specific demographics.

03

Male Empowerment

Dedicated programming to engage men in health, wellness, and family life — breaking barriers and building culture around self-care.

04

Youth Development

Mentorship, skills training, and recreational programming for young people to build confidence, character, and community ties.

05

Counselling & Mental Wellness

Prayer support, professional counselling, and substance misuse intervention delivered with compassion and without judgment.

06

Digital Access & Inclusion

Internet service booths and digital literacy support to bridge the connectivity gap in rural and underserved communities.

Flagship Programme

Operation
Grace

Launched in response to the devastation caused by Hurricane Melissa, Operation Grace is NYFDF's flagship community recovery programme — a six-month series of monthly outreach events serving affected communities across Jamaica.

Each edition is designed around a specific community need — from general health screening to male empowerment, women's wellness, and youth engagement. Cambridge St. James is our current home for 2026.
